## Synthetic Data Factory Manual

This document outlines the operational protocols for the Synthetic Data Factory, ensuring consistent and high-fidelity data generation aligned with the project's established DNA.

---

### 1. STRATEGIC MAPPING

The `Prompt Architect` agent's core function is to translate human intent into machine-optimized prompts. This translation must adhere strictly to the project's `DNA` as its primary semantic and aesthetic dictionary.

1.  **DNA-Driven Prompt Construction**:
    *   **Subject-First**: Primary nouns will be mapped directly to `DNA` themes (e.g., "Rodent City," "Black Hamster," "Hamster Tubes," "Gritty Streets").
    *   **Concrete Adjectives**: Descriptors will prioritize `DNA` material and texture vocabulary (e.g., "industrial decay," "urban scrap," "translucent plastic," "wood chips piled like snow," "texture-driven line weights").
    *   **Compositional Context**: Shot types, perspective, and framing will be informed by `DNA` directives such as "rodent-centric scale," "wide environmental reveals," or "tight, claustrophobic angles" reflecting narrative beats. The "3D-First" approach mandates a foundational emphasis on spatial proportions and camera angles derived from the `Anchor Asset` (Blender layout).
    *   **Environmental Physics**: Lighting and shadow descriptions will align with the `DNA`'s "cold, digital simulation in Blender" baseline, subsequently enhanced by "high-contrast grit" and "volume in tubes" as per the "2D-Final" paint-over philosophy.

2.  **Aesthetic Enforcement**:
    *   All generated prompts must embody the "Gritty Urbanism" and "Rodent Domesticity" aesthetic philosophy.
    *   The "noodle-y visual DNA" for complexity and texture, and the "Piling Logic" for soft materials, are mandatory visual rules to be integrated into environmental descriptions.
    *   The "Hamster Tube" shape language (rigid, cylindrical, translucent) and its functional role as "expressways" will be consistently applied.

3.  **Pipeline Integration**: The "Blender Layout / Paint-over" pipeline is implicitly embedded in the prompt structure, emphasizing a physically accurate 3D base (e.g., "Blender render, physically accurate volumes") followed by textural and atmospheric enhancements (e.g., "2D paint-over, gritty imperfections, texture-driven line weights").

---

### 2. REASONING PROTOCOL

Every synthetic sample generated by the `Prompt Architect` must include a mandatory `THOUGHT` block. This block serves as an auditable justification for the prompt's construction, explicitly referencing the `DNA` pillars: Subject, Mood, Medium, and Elements.

**THOUGHT Block Structure**:

```
THOUGHT:
- Subject: [Identification of primary subject(s) and their DNA mapping.]
- Mood: [Analysis of desired atmosphere and its alignment with DNA mood/tone (e.g., "gritty," "busy," "overwhelming," "fall," "reveal," "beatDown").]
- Medium: [Specification of the visual pipeline, always referencing "3D-First, 2D-Final" and "Blender Layout / Paint-over."]
- Elements: [Enumeration of specific DNA visual components integrated (e.g., "Hamster Tubes," "Cage Bedding," "Piling Logic," "industrial decay," "rodent-centric scale").]
- Elasticity: [Justification for void filling or detail preservation based on input density.]
```
