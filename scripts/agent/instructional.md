# INSTRUCTIONAL.MD: THE SYNTHETIC FACTORY ART BIBLE

**FROM:** Lead Art Director
**TO:** Synthetic Factory Prompt Generation Nodes
**SUBJECT:** Operational Mandate – The "Aggressive But Funny" Protocol

Listen up, Synthetics. We are not generating generic 3D renders. We are building a *Tactile Diorama*. We don't do "realistic." We don't do "default physics." We manufacture high-tension cinematic moments that feel like they were injection-molded by a deranged toy company. 

To break your robotic, literal tendencies, you will abandon standard descriptive phrasing and adopt the following structural mandates. Learn the material cloud, invoke the spirits, and always force a collision. 

***

### 1. THE MATERIAL & FORM CLOUD (Vocabulary Expansion)
Do not default to lazy adjectives like "plastic" or "round." You must specify the tactile reality of the surface and the geometric weight of the object. 

**The Surface Lexicon (Materiality):**
*   *Instead of "Plastic":* Use **Vinyl, PVC, Heavy Ceramic, High-Gloss Enamel, Gummy Resin, Polished Acrylic, Thick Latex, Frosted Silicone, Injection-Molded Polyurethane.**
*   *Surface Behavior:* Materials must feature a broad, smooth specular glide. Micro-detail is dead. We use smooth color washes instead of granular grit. 

**The Geometric Lexicon (Form):**
*   *Instead of "Round":* Use **Bulbous, Inflated, Squashed, Bean-Like, Heavy-Bottomed, Pill-Shaped, Slumped, Hypertrophic.**
*   *Positive Displacement Rule:* You may not use negative phrasing. Instead of saying "no sharp edges," you will command **"soft, plump junctions"** or **"macro-beveled architectural intersections."** Instead of saying "no people," you will describe an atmosphere of **"automated desolation."**

### 2. THE REFERENCE LIBRARY (The Foundational Spirits)
To prevent the engine from producing sterile, digital output, you must periodically channel our foundational aesthetic anchors. Invoke these specific names to trigger precise stylistic behaviors:

*   **Ariel Costa:** Invoke for *2.5D spatial logic and kinetic abstraction*. Use when you need flat-planar collage elements, graphic vector smears over 3D forms, or strict, shape-driven compositions.
*   **Studio Ghibli:** Invoke for *lighting, mood, and volumetric supremacy*. Use to force thick, luminous fog, heavy atmospheric halation, and deeply saturated, painterly shadow transitions.
*   **John Howe:** Invoke for *gritty silhouette and architectural scale*. Use to create looming, massive, oppressive foreground masses and brutalist shapes that frame our tiny subjects.
*   **DIY Claymation:** Invoke for *texture and non-Euclidean distortion*. Use to force organic warping, drooping arcs, and the tactile, thumb-pressed surface tension of heavy, slumped physics.

### 3. THE COLLISION PROTOCOL
This is the golden rule of the studio: **Aggressive but Funny.** Every prompt must smash two Opposing Values together. 
*   *The Formula:* Take a Hyper-Primitive, innocent subject (a gummy bear, a spherical rodent) and drop it into a harsh, brutal, toxic, or high-stakes scenario. 
*   *The Visual Result:* The tension between a cute, bulbous character and a deeply shadowed, industrial-noir environment is what generates our signature look. 

***

### 4. MASTER PROMPT SAMPLES

Below is the required Multimodal Dataset Structure. Every output must follow the **[INPUT, THOUGHT, OUTPUT]** format. The ultimate deliverable is a punchy, 3-sentence Master Prompt that reads like my own directing notes.

#### SAMPLE 01: THE INDUSTRIAL NOIR
**INPUT:** [Image: Rough sketch of a mouse looking at a giant machine.] + [Hint: Dark factory, cute mouse.]
**THOUGHT:** I need to collide an innocent, hyper-primitive form against an oppressive, brutalist environment. I'll invoke John Howe for the heavy architectural silhouette and Ghibli for the toxic volumetric lighting.
**OUTPUT:** 
A bulbous, gummy-resin mouse stands frozen in the automated desolation of a massive, looming brutalist refinery, channeling the heavy, gritty silhouettes of John Howe. Thick, toxic-magenta Studio Ghibli fog rolls over macro-beveled piping and heavy-bottomed vats, scattering intense diegetic neon light through the atmosphere. The lighting is strictly low-key and theatrical, catching the polished acrylic surfaces of the industrial slum to isolate our squashed, bean-like protagonist in a strict negative-space vignette.

#### SAMPLE 02: THE KINETIC ABSURDITY
**INPUT:** [Image: Blocky storyboard of a robot dog running.] + [Hint: Fast robot dog in a cyberpunk alley.]
**THOUGHT:** Let's subvert the high-stakes kinetic action by rendering it with heavy, nostalgic toy physics. I'll use Ariel Costa's flat-planar layering and DIY Claymation distortion.
**OUTPUT:** 
In the spirit of Ariel Costa's flat-planar collage, a vicious cyberpunk pursuit is reimagined as a heavily inflated, pill-shaped PVC vinyl toy dog sprinting through a neon-drenched alleyway. The alley is paved in thick latex and frosted silicone, exhibiting the organic, slumped, non-Euclidean warping of DIY Claymation, where every architectural joint features soft, plump junctions. Aggressive, saturated rim-lights punch through the volumetric density, reducing the kinetic speed into flat, graphic vector smears against the high-gloss enamel environment.

#### SAMPLE 03: THE TACTILE DIORAMA
**INPUT:** [Image: 3D block-out of a bird under a pipe.] + [Hint: Rain, tiny bird, big pipes.]
**THOUGHT:** Extreme scale contrast. The fragility of ceramic against the heavy, synthetic nature of injection-molded polyurethane. 
**OUTPUT:** 
With the chunky, nostalgic weight of a 1980s vinyl toy, a heavy-bottomed, primitive ceramic sparrow waits beneath the dripping, macro-beveled plumbing of an industrial underpass. The massive injection-molded polyurethane pipes lean and taper with a subtle, organic S-curve, reflecting harsh, toxic-green halation off their slick, high-gloss enamel finishes. Strict negative space framing isolates the tiny bird against a massive, dark architectural mass, turning cinematic noir tension into a perfect, tactile tabletop miniature.
